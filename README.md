# AI Lead Generation & Outreach System

An AI-powered workflow designed to help businesses discover potential customers, organize prospect information, and streamline their outreach process.

## The Problem

Finding suitable prospects, researching companies, organizing contact information, and preparing personalized outreach can take considerable time.

This system is designed to reduce repetitive research and administrative work through workflow automation.

## Key Features

- AI-assisted company and prospect research
- Lead information organized into a structured pipeline
- Contact information collected when publicly verifiable
- Personalized outreach message preparation
- Google Sheets integration for lead management
- Automated outreach workflow
- Outreach status tracking and activity logging

## How It Works

1. A lead research request initiates the research workflow.
2. An AI agent researches potential companies and gathers relevant information.
3. Results are structured into consistent fields.
4. Qualified leads are organized in Google Sheets.
5. The outreach workflow processes eligible leads.
6. Outreach activity and status changes are recorded for tracking.

## Technology Stack

- n8n — Workflow automation
- AI language models — Prospect research and outreach preparation
- Google Sheets — Lead pipeline and activity tracking
- Email integration — Outreach delivery

## Human Oversight

Prospect information should be reviewed for accuracy before use.

Outreach should follow applicable privacy, marketing, and email requirements. Automated sending should be restricted to eligible, appropriately approved leads.

## Project Status

Portfolio project demonstrating AI-assisted research, data organization, and business outreach automation.

## Important Note

This project does not guarantee lead accuracy, customer acquisition, or sales results. Contact information and research findings should be verified before business use.
